How will you rate housing options?

I'll be using three arbitrary categories: Location, Atmosphere, and Utility.

Each rating category will have a rank from D to S. S indicates that it's the best (or tied for best), and D indicates that it's the worst. The order from worst to best goes D-C-B-A-S.

When it comes to location, closer is better. Closer to classes, the gym, the dining hall, parking lots, just about anything. Since location importance is debatable (e.g. is being closer to classes more important than the dining hall?) it's important to determine which locations are considered more important and less important. These ratings are meant for the general student, so not an RA or an unusual student who doesn't ever go to class. Therefore, I will consider class location as the most important, with everything else supporting this category.

Atmosphere describes the environment that the dorm provides. This can be separated further into 1. physical atmosphere (meaning aesthetics mostly since HVAC is in Utilities) and 2. social atmosphere (meaning proximity to other students, aptitude for social gatherings, proximity to parties, etc.)

Utility describes how useful the physical dorm is for enhancing your productivity and giving the resident direct, quantifiable options to thrive. This means space, convenience, etc., all within the dorm itself. This describes things like its HVAC, study areas, kitchen, the beds, how you navigate within the dorm, where its mailroom is, etc.

Alderman Hall-style Dorms

Also known as "new dorms", these are the most recently built (not renovated) dorms for first-years on grounds.

Residents live in doubles, meaning two residents per room, where there is a common bathroom for all the residents of a floor.

Advantages of new dorms:

  • Closest to O-Hill Dining Hall (best breakfast on grounds) and Slaughter Gym. Most of the dorms are also closest to the AFC, which is the best gym for most students on Grounds
  • Up to six stories high depending on the building, if you get a high floor you will probably have a nice view from your window
  • Most modern-looking from the outside
  • Tons of outdoor space for activities interspersed around. The number of buildings and their distances create their own "campus" of sorts which makes for a good environment
  • Large common areas suitable for studying on each floor, first floors have very large common spaces and kitchen areas for cooking/eating
  • Very secure, primarily use elevators which are well-maintained and are keycard-access only.
    •

    Disadvantages of new dorms:

  • Difference in quality varies heavily depending on the building
  • Hall-style communal bathrooms are worse than old dorms'
    •

    Each building is different. There are a total of nine different buildings in new dorms, which all have their advantages and disadvantages, some greater than others.

    Balz-Dobie, "Balz"

    This dorm is almost exclusively for Echols and Rodman Scholars. Therefore, most of the students here are nerds, of the good and of the bad varieties.

    If someone says they live in Balz, you probably won't be surprised.

    I stayed in this dorm for my orientation and it wasn't amazing. The lights didn't really work, the interior is bland and the bed smelled like sweat for some reason.

    The beds are all pretty shitty on grounds to be honest, so I'm not gonna hold that against Balz.

    Balz is cool because they do cool Echols/Rodman scholar things, I've seen workshops that Rodman scholars do where they have fun cooking activties and such.

    However, since they're all nerds, there isn't really any community here for students looking to party. Most residents are antisocial or aren't interested in going to large social gatherings.

    If you're an Echols or Rodman Scholar and you're looking for a place to find a special community of driven people, Balz is definitely the place to go to.

    However, if you're an Echols or Rodman Scholar and you lowkey just want to party and don't do well hanging out with introverted people, I would look somewhere else.

    The location isn't great but isn't terrible for a dorm, being second-furthest away after Kellogg.

    Room size: 16' x 11'6"

    Ratings:

  • Location: B
  • Atmosphere: A- (Echols and Rodman Scholars thrive here)
  • Utility: A-

    Gibbons

    This dorm is in a seriously good spot, a 30 second walk to the AFC.

    This dorm is distinguished because it houses most of the first-year athletes since it's the closest hall-style dorm to the AFC and the stadium.

    The interior and exterior is probably the best out of any of the new dorms, although most of them aren't really that different from each other.

    Given that the dorm is filled with a ton of athletes, the social atmosphere in here is going to be pretty great, that goes without saying.

    Like most of the other new dorms (except Woody & Cauthen ofc) it has great open study spaces on each floor and is elevator-powered with keycard access, so it's secure like Balz.

    Room size: 16' x 11'6"

    Ratings:

  • Location: A
  • Atmosphere: A
  • Utility: A-
    •

    Kellogg

    I prefer to refer to Kellogg as "Mt. Kellogg" due to the number of stairs you need to climb just to enter it. It's located on top of Observatory Hill, the topological feature, not the dining hall.

    It's definitely the furthest away from everything out of all of the new dorms as well. However, because it's so high up and far away, you can get really good views from your room if it's facing towards Grounds.

    Other than that, this dorm is about the same as most of the other new dorms.

    Room size: 15'11" x 11'7"

    Ratings:

  • Location: C
  • Atmosphere: B
  • Utility: B+

    Lile-Maupin and Tuttle-Dunnington, "Lile" and "Tuttle"

    These two buildings' locations are really convenient. They are both the closest to O-Hill as well as Slaughter and a convenient bus stop.

    They also have the largest rooms out of all of new dorms. The interior isn't too shabby, either.

    These dorms are grouped together because they have the same sized rooms and because there's nothing else really distinguishing them from other new dorms.

    Room size: 17'10" x 11'8"

    Ratings:

  • Location: A-
  • Atmosphere: B
  • Utility: A

    Shannon

    This building is functionally closest to Gibbons, it's fairly central in the new dorms complex.

    It's got just as nice of an interior as any of the other good new dorms and is fairly squeaky clean.

    There's also a chance you'll have a class on the first floor of the building, there's like 4 small classrooms there.

    The rooms aren't quite as large as Lile and Tuttle, but they're still larger than the other new dorms rooms, so it's pretty damn nice.

    Ratings:

    Alderman Suite-style Dorms (Motels)

    Better known as the "motels" because they are long and parallel to the road with covered outdoor walkways leading to each suite, so they look like motels.

    They are located along Alderman Rd adjacent to New Dorms and the stadium towards Gooch/Dillard. It's honestly not a horrible location, although it can be quite the walk.

    There are three of these: Courtenay, Dunglison ("Dungle"), and Fitzhugh ("Fitzy"). They aren't different from each other in any way other than location, although the quality will vary year to year randomly.

    These dorms have 8-10 residents, usually 10, with five double-occupancy rooms. There is a communal bathroom in each suite with two toilets, two showers, and a triple vanity.

    There is a massive living area in each suite, making it extremely good for pregames, watch parties, and other gatherings.

    Because you live with so many people, the potential for an active social environment is off the charts.

    The main drawback of living here is that one in every two suites has an RA living in one of the bedrooms, which makes for an awkward living scenario since alcohol isn't technically allowed in first-year dorms.

    However, if your RA is chill or doesn't care, you've basically hit the jackpot - you just need to be careful for RA Coverage nights since every RA on coverage technically needs to enter each suite to check them.

    Room size: 16' x 12'

    Ratings:

  • Location: B-
  • Atmosphere: S
  • Utility: B
